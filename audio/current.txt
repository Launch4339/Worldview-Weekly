TITLE: Worldview Weekly — Audio Test

Welcome to Worldview Weekly. This is a short test of the audio edition: a calm, low-noise weekly briefing designed to complement The Economist with a broader range of reputable international perspectives. Each Thursday, the full edition will turn the week’s selected articles into an original spoken briefing, organized by subject rather than by publication. The narration uses a neutral American English voice. If this voice feels right, the same voice will be used for future editions.
